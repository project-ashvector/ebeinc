--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Looping 16:9 ALLTHINGS140 visuals video screen with rounded corners.
- Video playback engine:
  * Hardware-accelerated isolated video renderer (VisualsVideoView).

    switching to the Green Room Chat tab, resuming seamlessly on return.
  * Prevents memory leaks and multiple player instances.
