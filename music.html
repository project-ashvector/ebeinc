Professional audio experience

From empty session to finished master.

My experience is not limited to releasing tracks. It includes the repeated technical decisions required to make dense music clear, controlled, impactful, and ready for different listening environments.

01

Production & arrangement

Song structure, synthesis, sample selection, MIDI programming, editing, transitions, energy control, and session organization.

02

Mix engineering

Gain staging, EQ, compression, saturation, routing, spatial design, automation, low-end management, and revision workflows.

03

Mastering

Final tonal and dynamic shaping, loudness management, quality control, translation checks, and release-ready file preparation.

04

Artist operations

Catalog management, branding, websites, promotional planning, visual assets, release coordination, and audience-facing presentation.
