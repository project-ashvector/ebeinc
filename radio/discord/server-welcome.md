# Welcome to AllThings140 Radio

Welcome to the official AllThings140 Radio community — a public home for heavy dubstep, riddim, 140 BPM music, live DJ takeovers, and the listeners who keep the transmission moving.

## Start here

- **#welcome** — read the rules and introduce yourself.
- **#radio-announcements** — live show alerts, station news, and schedule updates.
- **#now-playing** — current track and listener reactions.
- **#listener-lounge** — talk music, share discoveries, and meet the community.
- **#track-submissions** — learn how to submit music for station review.
- **#dj-and-artist-corner** — releases, mixes, collaborations, and event posts.

## Listen live

Open the synchronized station at https://allthings140radio.online/

## Community rules

Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, spam, scams, or unsolicited promotion. Keep shared links safe for the community and follow moderator direction. This is a music-first space — help keep it welcoming and loud.
