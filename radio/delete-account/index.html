Delete your ALLTHINGS140 account

ALLTHINGS140 account holders can request deletion of their account and associated personal account data.

Important: this page submits an authenticated deletion request. A secure server-side processor completes destructive Auth, profile, avatar, and related-data cleanup; the browser never receives a privileged key.

How to request deletion

  1. Sign in to your ALLTHINGS140 account in this browser.
  2. Open your Profile or Account settings.
  3. Select the account deletion option.
  4. Confirm the deletion request below.

The request removes or de-identifies applicable authentication, profile, avatar, and other associated personal account data. Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or dispute handling.
