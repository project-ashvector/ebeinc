# Operational legal/compliance checklist

This is operational planning, not legal advice. Before scale or monetization, obtain qualified guidance on music performance/mechanical licensing, artist-submission rights, advertising disclosures, privacy and analytics disclosures, website terms, support/payment language, subscription cancellation/refund terms, email consent, Google Play/Android Auto/Automotive policies, and future App Store/CarPlay requirements. Recheck requirements by listener territory and do not treat a technically public stream as proof of licensing compliance.
